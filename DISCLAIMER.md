# Disclaimer

Redefluss is an unofficial, experimental learning tool. Please read this before you rely on it.

## Not official

Redefluss is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Goethe-Institut, telc, the BAMF, any textbook publisher, or the makers of any app it works with. Exam names such as Goethe-Zertifikat are trademarks of their owners and appear here only to say what the tool practises for. Exam formats, timings and pass marks described in this repository were checked against public material in 2026 and can change. The exam provider's current official information always takes precedence.

## AI feedback can be wrong

The teaching is done by whichever AI model you connect. It can misjudge an answer, give a wrong correction or explanation, or agree with a mistake. The engine adds checks where it can (answer keys, a second judge for free use, a human teacher's blind ratings), but most marks are still the model's judgement. Marks for writing and speaking are estimates, not exam results. The levels the tool reports are a record of practice, not a CEFR placement or a certificate.

## No guarantee

Using Redefluss does not guarantee that you pass an exam or reach a level. Plans such as "B2 in 150 days" are targets, not promises. The software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind, and you use it at your own risk.

## Your data

Your learner database (profile, answers, mistakes, transcripts of your speech, scores) is a file on your own computer, `data/private/tutor.db`. It is git-ignored and never part of this repository.

The lessons themselves happen in an AI app. What you say and write in a lesson, and what the engine's tools return to the model, is sent to that app's provider and handled under its terms. Don't share anything in a lesson that you wouldn't share with them.

## Third-party material

Textbooks, their answer keys and their audio are copyrighted. Keep your own extractions in `data/book/`, which is git-ignored, and don't publish them. Data you download for the tool, such as a frequency list, keeps its own licence (see the README).
